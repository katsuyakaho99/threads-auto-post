# Summary of Research and Findings — The Westin Yokohama

_Research compiled: 2026-07-13_

## Overview

The Westin Yokohama is a premium lifestyle **wellness (well-being) hotel** that
opened in **June 2022** in the Minato Mirai district of Yokohama, Japan. Operated
under Marriott's Westin brand (Marriott Bonvoy), it positions well-being at the
center of the guest experience, combining spacious accommodations, a full
wellness floor, and multiple dining venues.

- **Brand / operator:** Westin — Marriott Bonvoy
- **Opened:** June 2022
- **Address:** 4-2-8 Minato Mirai, Nishi-ku, Yokohama, Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan
  (神奈川県横浜市西区みなとみらい4-2-8)
- **Concept:** Well-being / wellness lifestyle hotel

## Rooms & Suites

- **373 rooms and suites** in total.
- Every room is **42 m² or larger**, ranging up to roughly **210 m²** for top suites.
- Floor-to-ceiling windows with views of **Yokohama Bay** or **Mount Fuji** from
  select rooms.
- All rooms feature Westin's signature new-generation **Heavenly® Bed** (plush
  pillow-top mattress, oversized duvet, cooling silver-weave top).
- Higher room categories include **Club Lounge** access.

## Dining

The hotel offers roughly **four to five restaurants and bars**, emphasizing
seasonal, locally sourced ingredients:

- **Iron Bay** — signature restaurant featuring hearty wood-fired dishes.
- **Brasserie du Quai** — 3rd-floor venue known for a luxurious breakfast.
- **Waterline** — modern Yokohama izakaya-style dining.
- Additional bar/lounge outlets round out the lineup.

## Wellness & Facilities

A dedicated wellness floor is a defining feature of the property:

- **Heavenly Spa by Westin** — the first spa in Japan to carry the "Heavenly
  Spa by Westin" name; scented with white tea, with **4 treatment rooms**
  including **2 couples' rooms**.
- **Indoor pool** — naturally lit, **20 m** long, with a jet bath. Open 8:00–20:00
  (guests under 15: 10:00–18:00).
- **Fitness studio** — available **24 hours**.
- **Club Lounge** — a spacious **467 m²** lounge overlooking Minato Mirai.

## Location & Access

Centrally located in Minato Mirai, Yokohama's waterfront business and tourism
district, with strong transit connectivity:

- **~45 minutes by train from Tokyo Station.**
- **~25 minutes by car from Haneda (Tokyo International) Airport.**
- Nearest stations: **Minato Mirai**, **Sakuragicho**, and **Yokohama** — all
  within walkable access.

### Nearby Attractions

- Landmark Tower (~10-minute walk)
- Yokohama Anpanman Children's Museum
- Cup Noodles Museum Yokohama
- Yokohama Museum of Art
- Sankeien Garden (historic Japanese garden)

## Pricing

- Indicative rates start around **¥30,000 per room per night**, varying by season,
  room category, and demand.

## Key Takeaways

- Newest-generation Westin wellness hotel in Yokohama's Minato Mirai (opened 2022).
- Large, view-oriented rooms (all 42 m²+) with signature Heavenly Beds.
- Strong wellness proposition: Japan's first Heavenly Spa by Westin, 20 m indoor
  pool, and a 24-hour fitness studio.
- Excellent access to Tokyo, Haneda, and Yokohama's main sightseeing spots.
